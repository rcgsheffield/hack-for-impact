# Voiceover script

Each `## <id>` section is one voiceover clip. `tools/tts.py` sends the text under each heading to ElevenLabs and writes `composition/assets/voice/<id>.mp3`. Lines starting with `>` are direction notes and are not spoken.

Draft lines: rewrite them once the demo is settled.

## s1
> Scene 01, Irene and the phone. Calm, slightly weary.

This is Irene. There's a pothole on her street, and she wants the council to fix it.

## s2
> Scene 02, form X.

She rings up. Twenty minutes on hold, and she's told to fill in form X. Then form Y.

## s3
> Scene 03, overwhelmed council worker.

Meanwhile, at the council, every report lands in the same inbox. Hundreds of them. Every day.

## s4
> Scene 04, the pause. Slow, dramatic.

If only there was another way.

## s5
> Scene 05, chat window. Upbeat.

Now Irene just describes the problem, in her own words. Our agent asks what it needs to know, and files the report for her.

## s6
> Scene 06, agent flow infographic. Clear and confident.

Behind the scenes, the agent works out which service is responsible, checks council open data for existing reports, fills in the right system, and keeps Irene updated. And it all runs locally, on an NVIDIA DGX Spark.

## s7
> Scene 07, close.

Less time on hold for residents. Less time triaging for the council. This is CouncilTriagent.
