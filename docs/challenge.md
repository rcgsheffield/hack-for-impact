# Hack for Impact: challenge brief

- **Event:** https://events.nvidia.com/hack-for-impact-sheffield/
- **Date:** Friday 13 November 2026, 09:00 – 20:00
- **Theme:** Build autonomous systems that think, act, and run anywhere, whilst also making a positive impact on the world.

## Criteria

Submissions are evaluated on:

- **Impact**: Does the solution address a real problem?
- **Technical execution**: How well does it leverage agentic AI and local compute?
- **Feasibility**: Could this realistically be built or deployed beyond the hackathon?

## Challenge tracks

Each track describes a theme of impact, not the scope of your idea. Teams may build any solution that delivers against one of these themes.

### Economic Systems
**Focus:** Improving how money flows through businesses, workers, and markets.
**Goal:** Build agentic systems that help individuals and organisations make better economic decisions, unlock opportunities, or optimise costs.

### Public Services
**Focus:** Enhancing how people access and interact with public services and resources.
**Goal:** Use data to build tools that simplify navigation of public systems, making essential services more accessible, efficient, and user-friendly.

### Urban Operations
**Focus:** Optimising how cities run, from large-scale infrastructure to everyday life.
**Goal:** Develop systems that improve how cities function behind the scenes and in real time.

## Data

Public datasets are available if useful, including the Sheffield City Council Open Data portal (and its Sheffield Local Insight neighbourhood indicators), alongside national sources like data.gov.uk and the ONS. Teams are not required to use any particular data source.
