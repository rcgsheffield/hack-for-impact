    We can't help with

    • Private (unadopted) roads and car parks
    • Motorways, the A631 Tinsley Viaduct lower deck and the A616 Stocksbridge Bypass. Report these to National Highways.

    Other things to report

    • Faulty street lights
    • Traffic lights, crossings and road signs
    • Fly-tipping and graffiti

    The agent will redirect these if it spots them.
