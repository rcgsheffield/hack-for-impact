    What we do with your information

    The information you provide on this form will be used to process your request. We log your details on our Customer database so we have a record of your contact. Key details are then passed to the relevant officers so they can respond to your request.

    Sometimes we need to share your information with a third party. This might be because we’re working with another organisation, or we’ve contracted a company to deliver a service on our behalf. In these cases, we’ll only share the minimum information necessary.

    We keep your information in line with our retention policies. For further information about how long we keep information, how we process personal data, your rights under data protection, and contacting the Data Protection Officer, please see our Privacy Notice web page.

    If you choose to remain anonymous we won’t be able to update you with any action we take as the result of your request.

    What will happen next?

    We've sent your request to the Customer Services team. Our timescale for fixing this issue depends on the location and severity. We aim to address hazardous defects within 24 hours. Often this will be a temporary repair to make the area safe before we do a permanent repair later. The temporary repair might be in a different material.

    If we need any more information we will contact you. If we have everything we need, we won't update you when your request has been completed.
