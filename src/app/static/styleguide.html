CouncilTriagent styleguide

One look for chat, the triage console and the resident report page. Tokens are --ct-*, classes are ct-*.

Brand

Built on the team's smiling-tree logo: deep ring green for actions, leaf greens for the assistant, circuit gold for "local AI", bark brown sparingly. Never crests, crowns or GOV.UK styling.
